1. Tuesday, 7:30. The kitchen. Grey light. They are dressed for work, a cardigan under the jacket. The envelope from Chapter 3 is under the fruit bowl.
2. They know what line one of the back flap says without turning it over: "The Albion, by the station — until Friday." Daniel is in the city.
3. They make coffee with aggressive precision. The spoon hits the mug harder than it needs to.
4. They lift the fruit bowl, check the envelope is fully covered, set the bowl back down. Lift it again. Check again.
5. 7:40. The back door opens. Sarah lets herself in. She has a box of her own tea and asks where she can keep it.
6. They point at the middle dresser drawer, the empty one. She puts the box in.
7. Sarah asks about the weekend. They joke about her new project. It comes out too quick and lands wrong. They hear it land. They regret it and do not take it back.
8. Sarah: "You don't have to do that." She goes out to wait by the car.
9. Alone for thirty seconds. They take the envelope from under the fruit bowl and put it in the middle drawer, on top of Sarah's tea.
10. The key comes off the keyring for the first time in nine years. They lock the drawer. The key goes back on the ring.
11. The drive to work in silence. The radio is on. Neither of them hears it. They do not look out of the window once.
12. At lunch they pull up the hotel's website on their phone. They do not call. They clear the browser history, which takes longer than reading the letter would have.
