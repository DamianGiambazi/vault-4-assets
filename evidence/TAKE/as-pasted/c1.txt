1. Protagonist gets off the bus. Sarah is at the stop and walks beside them.
2. Sarah: "The garden gnome man. Did you see? He's sent a new photo. The gnome in front of the Eiffel Tower. As evidence of theft." Protagonist: "Evidence for whom?" Sarah: "For us. He wants it valued as well-travelled."
3. Both laugh.
4. Rain starts at the postbox. At the corner where their roads split, Sarah pushes her green umbrella into protagonist's hands.
5. Sarah: "Bring it tomorrow. I'm two minutes away." Sarah puts her hood up and leaves.
6. Protagonist walks the last hundred metres under the umbrella.
7. Protagonist finds post on the mat: gas statement, leaflet, charity envelope, cream envelope addressed in blue ink.
8. Protagonist picks up the cream envelope last, by the edges. The back reads: "Please, this time."
9. Protagonist stands in the hall with coat half off.
10. Protagonist puts the keys on the bare shelf, not in the blue dish. The umbrella drips in the stand.
11. Protagonist lays the envelope face down at the far end of the kitchen table, in front of the unused chair, and squares it to the edge.
12. Protagonist files the gas statement in the second drawer behind the Utilities divider.
13. Protagonist measures rice with a cup, levels it with a knife, dices onion and re-cuts uneven pieces, and wipes the counter twice in opposite directions. The radio stays off.
14. Protagonist eats dinner.
15. At eight, the window at number thirty-one stays dark.
16. At twenty past eight, protagonist is standing. They sit back down.
17. Protagonist gets up and pushes their chair in.
18. While drying up, protagonist knocks a cup against the tap. A hairline crack runs from the rim.
19. Protagonist holds the cup to the light and turns it.
20. Protagonist puts the cup down hard. It breaks into three pieces.
21. Protagonist makes a sound that is not a word.
22. Protagonist knocks the other cups off the rack onto the tiles.
23. Protagonist takes the envelope, opens the drawer, and begins to tear it open. They stop after about a centimetre.
24. Protagonist sits on the kitchen floor beside a piece of cup handle and cries. The light is on and the blind is up.
25. Protagonist sweeps the pieces into a pile and starts wrapping them in newspaper.
26. A knock at the door. Protagonist stays kneeling, holding the wrapped pieces. The drawer is open and the torn envelope is on the table.
27. A second knock. Sarah, through the door: "It's only me. Sorry, it's raining again, and it's my only one."
28. Protagonist gets up.
29. Protagonist opens the door halfway and stands in the gap. Sarah is on the step, hood up, rain on her glasses, looking at protagonist's face.
30. Sarah: "I saw your light." Protagonist: "I got a letter today. From someone I haven't heard from in fifteen years."
31. Sarah: "Have you read it?" Protagonist: "No." Sarah nods once.
32. Protagonist hands Sarah the umbrella. Neither moves.
33. Sarah: "Okay. Tomorrow, then."
34. Sarah goes down the path and closes the gate quietly.
35. Protagonist shuts the door and leans against it.
36. Protagonist bins the wrapped pieces and wipes the floor.
37. Protagonist smooths the tear flat, puts the envelope in the drawer under the boiler warranty, and closes the drawer.
38. Protagonist moves the keys from the shelf to the dish.
39. The kitchen has no cups.
40. Protagonist turns off the light at half past nine.
41. After midnight, protagonist goes downstairs in the dark and stops in the kitchen doorway.
42. The window at number thirty-one is lit low. A shape stands by the curtain.
43. Protagonist looks at the shape, then at their own raised blind and the street lamp shining in.
44. Protagonist does not lower the blind and stays standing in the doorway.
